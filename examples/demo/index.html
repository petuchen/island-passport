示範小島 Demo Island

Any website can join: one script tag adds a ✈ Routes button with a departures board, a take-off animation, and a welcome for travellers who arrive with ?from=…&by=…&via=….

Try arriving: as a traveller from Lighthouse Island.

Any vehicle works (1.2): arrive by helicopter, by balloon or by rocket.

Or arrive with a character (a traveller card): the welcome uses its name, and the return trip carries it home.
